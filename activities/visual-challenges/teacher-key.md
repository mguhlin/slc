# Science Visual Challenges: Teacher Key

Five separate challenge posters for each of grades 6, 7, and 8. These original classroom questions align with selected Texas science TEKS from the 2021 adoption, implemented in 2024–2025. They introduce or review parts of the cited expectations; they do not cover each expectation in full.

Source: [TEA: Science TEKS, Middle School](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/sboe-rules-tac/sboe-tac-currently-effect/ch112b.pdf). Alignment checked October 3, 2026.

## Classroom routine

Give students a minute to choose independently. Ask them to explain their evidence to a partner, then discuss an alternative answer. Use the follow-up question to check whether they can apply the idea in a different situation.

Students can use: “I choose ___ because ___. The evidence is ___.”

Read printed numbers as the given data. Illustrations represent simplified scenarios, not scale drawings. For the acceleration challenge, treat the displayed masses as total moving system masses. For the closed-system mass challenge, use the ideal classroom model and disregard buoyancy and measurement effects.

## Grade 6

| Image | Challenge | TEKS | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Which block will sink? | 6.6D | **C** |
| 02 | Which ball stores the most gravitational energy? | 6.8A | **B** |
| 03 | Which way will the canoe move? | 6.7C | **A** |
| 04 | Which rock formed from cooling lava? | 6.10C | **C** |
| 05 | Which pair shows mutualism? | 6.12B | **A** |

### 1. Which block will sink?

**Answer: C.** The solid block labeled 1.3 g/cm³ is denser than water and sinks; the other blocks are less dense than water.

**Follow-up:** What would change if the liquid had a density of 1.4 g/cm³?

### 2. Which ball stores the most gravitational energy?

**Answer: B.** With mass and gravity held constant, gravitational potential energy increases with height. B is highest at 4 m.

**Follow-up:** How could a lower ball store more energy than B?

### 3. Which way will the canoe move?

**Answer: A.** The paddle pushes water backward; the water simultaneously pushes the paddle and canoe forward. These interaction forces act on different objects.

**Follow-up:** Identify the two objects that exert the interaction forces.

### 4. Which rock formed from cooling lava?

**Answer: C.** Basalt is an igneous rock that can form when lava cools at Earth's surface. Sandstone and shale are sedimentary; marble is metamorphic.

**Follow-up:** What process could change sandstone into a metamorphic rock?

### 5. Which pair shows mutualism?

**Answer: A.** In this example, the bee obtains food and the flower receives pollination. The other scenes represent parasitism, predation, and a typical commensalism example.

**Follow-up:** Explain how mutualism differs from commensalism.

## Grade 7

| Image | Challenge | TEKS | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Which sugar sample dissolves fastest? | 7.6E | **D** |
| 02 | Which rover has the greatest average speed? | 7.7A | **C** |
| 03 | Which handle warms fastest by conduction? | 7.8A | **A** |
| 04 | Which change helps protect the stream? | 7.11A | **B** |
| 05 | Which example shows artificial selection? | 7.13D | **A** |

### 1. Which sugar sample dissolves fastest?

**Answer: D.** For sugar under these conditions, warmer water, smaller pieces, and stirring each increase the rate of dissolving. D combines all three.

**Follow-up:** Design a fair test of temperature alone.

### 2. Which rover has the greatest average speed?

**Answer: C.** A averages 5 m/s, B 4 m/s, C 9 m/s, and D 6 m/s. C has the greatest average speed.

**Follow-up:** How far would C travel in 5 seconds at that average speed?

### 3. Which handle warms fastest by conduction?

**Answer: A.** Among these materials, copper conducts thermal energy most readily, so its handle warms fastest when size and conditions are matched.

**Follow-up:** Which material would you choose for a handle meant to stay cooler?

### 4. Which change helps protect the stream?

**Answer: B.** A vegetated buffer can slow runoff, trap sediment, and take up some nutrients before they reach the stream.

**Follow-up:** What evidence would show whether the buffer is helping?

### 5. Which example shows artificial selection?

**Answer: A.** The gardener chooses which plants reproduce based on a desired heritable trait. Human choice drives artificial selection.

**Follow-up:** Why must the selected trait be heritable for this process to work?

## Grade 8

| Image | Challenge | TEKS | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | What will the scale read after the reaction? | 8.6E | **B** |
| 02 | Which cart has the greatest acceleration? | 8.7A | **C** |
| 03 | Which wave can help reveal a bone fracture? | 8.8B | **D** |
| 04 | Which setting best supports a hurricane? | 8.10C | **B** |
| 05 | Where would primary succession begin? | 8.12B | **C** |

### 1. What will the scale read after the reaction?

**Answer: B.** The whole closed system remains 150 g. Gas formation and atom rearrangement do not change its total mass when nothing enters or leaves.

**Follow-up:** What could make the measured mass decrease in an open version of this setup?

### 2. Which cart has the greatest acceleration?

**Answer: C.** The accelerations are A 2 m/s², B 1 m/s², C 4 m/s², and D 2 m/s². C has the greatest net force per unit mass.

**Follow-up:** How would doubling C's mass change its acceleration at the same net force?

### 3. Which wave can help reveal a bone fracture?

**Answer: D.** X-rays penetrate soft tissue more readily than bone, creating contrast that can help reveal a fracture on a radiograph.

**Follow-up:** Name a different application of electromagnetic waves.

### 4. Which setting best supports a hurricane?

**Answer: B.** Warm ocean water supplies energy and moisture; moist air and weak wind shear help an organized tropical cyclone develop when other necessary conditions are present.

**Follow-up:** Why can strong wind shear disrupt a developing storm?

### 5. Where would primary succession begin?

**Answer: C.** Primary succession starts on a surface without developed soil, such as newly cooled lava. The other scenes retain soil and represent recovery or ongoing disturbance.

**Follow-up:** How would soil remaining after a fire affect recovery?
